What it does

Reads your VA documents

Upload decision letters, C&P exams, rating decisions, SSOC — it reads them and tells you exactly what the VA said and why.

Spots what's missing

Identifies gaps in your evidence, missed nexus connections, and errors in the VA's reasoning that could strengthen your appeal.

Builds your action plan

As you talk, a live checklist builds on screen — what's done, what you still need, what forms to file.

Nexus letter drafts

If your case needs a doctor's opinion connecting your condition to service, we generate a ready-to-sign draft you bring to your physician.

Forms — filled out with you

We walk through the 21-526EZ, 10-182, and other VA forms section by section, collecting your answers, so you leave with everything ready to file.

MyVAAssist is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. For free representation, find a VSO at va.gov. Your documents are never stored — everything stays in your browser session only.
